# Axiom Tech - Internal Systems Terms of Use & Security Policy

## 1. Acceptable Use of Corporate Equipment & Systems
- All company-provided hardware (laptops, monitors) and digital tools (Slack, Google Workspace, GitHub, OCI console) are reserved exclusively for authorized business operations.
- Storing unencrypted personal data or pirated software on company assets is strictly prohibited.

## 2. Password & Access Governance
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory across all enterprise logins.
- Passwords must be at least 16 characters long and updated every 90 days.
- Access privileges follow the Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP).

## 3. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure (NDA)
- Proprietary source code, customer databases, product roadmaps, and internal metrics are classified as **Strictly Confidential**.
- Sharing internal documentation or repositories with external unauthorized parties constitutes immediate grounds for contract termination.
