# Axiom Tech - Privacy Policy & LGPD Compliance Guide

## 1. Scope & Commitment
Axiom Tech is fully committed to compliance with the General Data Protection Law (LGPD - Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, Law nº 13.709/2018). This policy applies to all employees, contractors, partners, and internal systems processing Personal Data (PD) and Sensitive Personal Data (SPD).

## 2. Legal Bases for Processing
- **Legal Obligation**: Compliance with labor, tax, and regulatory requirements.
- **Contract Execution**: Processing necessary for employment contracts and customer service agreements.
- **Legitimate Interest**: Maintaining system security, fraud prevention, and operational efficiency.
- **Consent**: Explicit consent collected for optional benefits, promotional media, or biometric authorization.

## 3. Data Subject Rights (Employees & Clients)
Data subjects have the right to request:
1. Confirmation of data processing and access to data.
2. Correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data.
3. Anonymization, blocking, or elimination of unnecessary or non-compliant data.
4. Data portability to another service provider.
5. Information regarding shared entities.

## 4. DPO Contact & Incident Notification
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**: `dpo@axiomtech.com`
- Any suspected data breach or security incident involving personal data MUST be reported to the DPO within **2 hours** of discovery.
