# Axiom Tech - Internal Communications & Slack Policy

## 1. Primary Channels & Purpose
- `#announcements`: Official company announcements (read-only for all staff).
- `#incident-war-room`: Real-time coordination for SEV-1 and SEV-2 operational outages.
- `#general`: General discussion, non-urgent company updates.
- `#tech-talks`: Knowledge sharing, architecture discussions, and tech news.
- `#hr-ask-me-anything`: Questions for HR, benefits, and workplace policies.

## 2. Communication Best Practices
- **Async First**: Default to asynchronous communication with detailed context and clear links.
- **Thread Discipline**: Keep replies inside Slack threads to prevent channel clutter.
- **Urgency Signals**: Use `@here` or `@channel` ONLY for active SEV-1 incidents or critical emergency alerts.
