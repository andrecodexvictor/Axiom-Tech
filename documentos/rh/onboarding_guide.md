# Axiom Tech - Employee Onboarding Guide

## 1. Welcome to Axiom Tech!
Congratulations on joining the team! This guide walks you through your first week at Axiom Tech.

## 2. First Week Checklist
- **Day 1**:
  - Attend Welcome Session with People Experience (HR) at 09:30 AM.
  - Complete 1Password setup and 2FA activation.
  - Receive MacBook Pro / Workstation setup from IT Ops.
- **Day 2**:
  - Meet your Team Onboarding Buddy.
  - Set up local development environment (Docker, Git credentials, Python/Go runtimes).
- **Day 3**:
  - Review Back-end / Front-end guidelines and microservice map.
  - Submit first test Pull Request (PR) to repository.
- **Day 4-5**:
  - Complete LGPD & Security awareness training module.
  - 1-on-1 alignment with your Engineering/Manager lead.
